Privacy

Short version: what you type into our tools stays on your device. We do not collect it.

What happens to the data you enter

  • The tools run entirely in your browser. Dates, names, passport numbers or anything else you enter are not sent to Plainsight Lab or anyone else.
  • The 90/180 calculator saves your trips in your browser's local storage so you find them next time. You can delete them at any time with “Delete all trips”, or by clearing your browser data. Personal details typed into the EES request generator are not saved.

Cookies and statistics

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. If we add audience statistics, we will use a cookieless, privacy-friendly service that counts page views without identifying you, and this page will name it.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Feedback

If you send feedback through GitHub issues, it is public and governed by GitHub's terms. Do not include personal or passport details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can contact the publisher (see the legal notice) about any data question, and complain to the CNIL (France).
