# Open data

## `ees-data-protection-contacts.(json|csv)`
Data controllers and supervisory authorities for the EU Entry/Exit System (EES), for the 29 participating countries — the authorities to write to for access to, or rectification of, your EES record (Article 52 of Regulation (EU) 2017/2226).

- **Source:** European Commission, [EES – contact details for data protection matters](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/ees/contact-details-for-data-protection-matters) (a JavaScript-only page, hard to read or search). Extracted 2026-10-22.
- **Licence:** the underlying information is published by the European Commission (reuse authorised, Decision 2011/833/EU). This compilation is released under CC0.
- Where a country lists several controllers, `primary: true` marks the main border authority. Spain and Hungary list no controller email; the DPO email is given with a note.
- Used by the [EES correction kit](https://mweber42.github.io/business-training/ees-record-wrong/).

Corrections welcome via issues.
