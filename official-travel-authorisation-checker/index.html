Already paid on an unofficial site?

  1. Do not pay anything more on that site, even if it asks for extra “processing fees”.
  2. Check whether an application was actually made for you: for the UK ETA use “Check an ETA” on gov.uk; for ESTA use “Check ESTA status” on the official site. If not, apply yourself on the official site.
  3. Contact your bank to dispute the payment if the service was not delivered or you were misled about it being official.
  4. Report the site to your national consumer or fraud authority.

How to spot an unofficial site

  • The address does not end with gov.uk (UK), cbp.dhs.gov (USA), canada.ca or gc.ca (Canada), europa.eu (EU).
  • The price is higher than the official price, or is split into “service + government fee”.
  • Small print says the site is “independent” or “not affiliated with the government”.
  • It appears at the top of search results marked “Sponsored”: ads are never a guarantee.
